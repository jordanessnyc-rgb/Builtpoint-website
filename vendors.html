04 How it works

From application
to first bid.

Pre-qualification is quick. Most vendors are in our bid list within two weeks of submitting.

01

Apply

Fill out the form below with your company, trade, and service area. It takes about five minutes.

02

Pre-qualify

We'll request your certificate of insurance, license, W-9, and two project references.

03

Onboard

Approved vendors sign our master subcontract or supply agreement and join the bid list.

04

Bid

You'll receive invitations to bid on projects that match your trade, size, and service area.
