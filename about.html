Builders

BuiltPoint Construction & Development is a general contractor and development partner delivering residential, commercial, and mixed-use projects with the discipline of a builder and the perspective of an owner.

02 Our story

Started on a job site, not in a boardroom.

BuiltPoint was founded by builders who spent years watching good projects go sideways because of handoffs: between estimators and field teams, between designers and trades, between the owner and everyone else.

We built the firm to close those gaps. Pre-construction, field supervision, and development management sit under one roof, with one person accountable for the result. Our name is a promise: get to the point, hit the point, and build the point right.
